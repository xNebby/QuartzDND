### About
The Bloodworn Cloak is a cult made of red-cloaked individuals that the party encountered in [Scorgate](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/%E2%99%A5%20Scorgate/Scorgate.md) outpost, when partaking in the [Escort the researchers](../../../../%F0%9F%95%AE%20Session%20Log/%F0%9F%93%91%20Quests/Scorgate/Escort%20the%20researchers.md) quest. The party were drugged by a member of their cult, Marie creek, who they found in the ruins of what looked like a bandit ambush on a caravan of travellers. 

The party were held hostage while one of the researchers, [Arianne Leyglade](../../Wizards/The%20researchers/Arianne%20Leyglade.md), was taken and forced to witness something horrific. This was the key event leading up to [The downfall of Scorgate](../../../../%F0%9F%95%AE%20Session%20Log/Sessions/Session%202.md).
