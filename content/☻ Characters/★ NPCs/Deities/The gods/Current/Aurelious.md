### About
Aurelious is often shown as a warm figure who brings joy to any they interact with. Most stories and myths of them show them in a younger form, often playing with others and making new games. Aurelious holds mortals in high regard, and is one of the more prominent gods in the dome due to how many sightings of them have been reported. Many do not know if all of these are factual due to the large number of them. 

Auraxia was originally brought into one continent under Aurelious' order, and was bequethed the name Auraxia- meaning "Continent born of the sun".
