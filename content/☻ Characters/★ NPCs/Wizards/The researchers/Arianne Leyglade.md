### About
A gloomy yet talented student of [Aurelia University](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/%E2%99%A5%20Archvale/POIs/Aurelia%20University.md). She made waves in last years mage tournament, where she single handedly bested mages twice her age in combat. Despite this, she prefers to work in the more theoretical fields- and strives to make magical artifacts that can help the everyday people. One of her graduating tasks was developing the heated faucet runes which are now commonplace over all of [Auraxia](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/Overview/Auraxia.md). She was also an assistant researcher that helped develop some of the groundbreaking magic eddie-current based generators, which got her the attention of [Sigril Blastpot](./Sigril%20Blastpot.md). 

Their department was shifted onto researching the worrying [Void-touched](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/Homebrew/Creatures/Void-touched.md) disease.

[Yvonne Astrea](../../Adventurer%20Groups/Aeternus'%20Party/Yvonne%20Astrea.md) had an eye on this student, and allowed her to partake in a research expedition despite her young age.

### Description
A girl on the shorter side. About 22 years old. She has beginners robes with a special gem on a necklace. 
Arianne initially appears shy and gloomy, but she has a passion for helping people and loves to discuss her work.
