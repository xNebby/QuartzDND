This group of researchers were sent by [Aurelia University](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/%E2%99%A5%20Archvale/POIs/Aurelia%20University.md) in order to research a potential spread vector of the [Void-touched](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/Homebrew/Creatures/Void-touched.md) disease.  They required assistance in the party's first quest [Escort the researchers](../../../../%F0%9F%95%AE%20Session%20Log/%F0%9F%93%91%20Quests/Scorgate/Escort%20the%20researchers.md). 
The researcher group was headed by the mage Sigril Blastpot and consisted of three mages, two students and two assistants. The party were to act as night watch to prevent the camels from being nabbed by any hungry desert critters. 

Researcher group:
- Head researcher - [Sigril Blastpot](./Sigril%20Blastpot.md)
- Researcher - Klein Heartwell 
- Researcher - Ella Furrow
- Student - [Arianne Leyglade](./Arianne%20Leyglade.md)
- Student - Cassi Carta
- Assistant - Mark Thrall
- Assistant - Austin Weatherby
