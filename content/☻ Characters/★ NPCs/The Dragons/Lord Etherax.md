Lord Etherax is the Dragon emperor, notable for inciting the [Revolution of Fire](Revolution%20of%20Fire.md). He commanded an army of dragons a 1,000 strong, and attempted to supplant the previous King of Auraxia [King Pleides](../../../../King%20Pleides.md).
