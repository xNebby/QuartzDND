### About
Brixburn is a simple god- he loves nothing more than to craft and smelt away. He tends not to get too entangled with the other gods, as he prefers to keep to a simple life. The other gods however often come to him in request of miraculous inventions. He often accepts, but only for a price he deems fair. 
Brixburn is often attributed to creating the Dwarves. He crafted the race in his own image, short, sturdy and logical thinkers who loved to create. He requested The goddess of life's help for this task, asking her to breathe life into his creations. She obliged, and simply asked him for a beautiful jewel in return- a gift for her wife, the goddess of death. 
Brixburn is also responsible for many of the initial holds that dwarves populated. Most notorious in the dome is [Cairnhold](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/%E2%99%A5%20Cairnhold/Cairnhold.md), a city handcrafted by the god- his pride being the incredible smelteries there, that even with mundane fuel could smelt the mightiest of metals. These smelteries also receive heat from the [Heart of a star](Heart%20of%20a%20star.md) that [Aurelious](./Aurelious.md) gave in return for Brixburn crafting the sun totem for the whale of [Moonveil](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/Moonveil/Moonveil.md). This Heart is capable of using magic as fuel, and allows the smelteries to run efficiently without any requirement for mundane resources- This does not eliminate the need for fuel however, as no matter how hot the star core runs it cannot ever hope to melt through Pure [Magisteel](../../../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Setting/Homebrew/Items/Magisteel.md).
