### Description:
Celaine is shorter than average for genasi, standing at around 5" tall at the age of 21. She is fairly pale as she has spent her days avoiding direct sunlight hiding away in libraries and under parasols. She dresses in fairly fancy clothing, primarily a crimson dress, with black corset and shoulder coverings, adorned with a silver necklace and often holding a black and crimson parasol on her left side. She has a slender build, with very little muscle, using magic to assist in physical labour. On her arms a physical manifestation of her curse is visible, as if her skin had been dipped in dark ink, higher concentration in the hands. Her face is round, and nicely framed by some soft and fluffy white hair that reaches down to her back, her ears stick out the side appearing similar to elven ears. She has a gentle expression most of the time, her eyes a soft shade of lilac, and has a spattering of small grey freckles over her nose.
