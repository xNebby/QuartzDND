### About
Rosewood is a druidic city built and maintained by The [Rosewood Circle](../../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Druids/Rosewood%20Circle.md). It is dedicated to keeping the peace and balance of nature around the forests and plains of [Auraxia](../../Overview/Auraxia.md) and is considered the most developed of the many Circle's groves. It has been built around the [World Tree](../../../../World%20Tree.md), which is an important figure in both the druid's worship and the balance of the local ecosystem.  

Rosewood has an outer section. This spans most of [Rosewood Forest](Rosewood%20Forest.md) and is where the common people who make a living in Rosewood live. The druids of rosewood live in the Inner grove area. This is a smaller area immediately around the [World Tree](../../../../World%20Tree.md), and includes a few mountains surrounding the tree. Many important buildings are housed inside the tree, being sewn into existence by the use of Tree whispering- a variation of Plant growth that over time instructs a tree to grow into a certain formation. This allowed the druids to make complex structures without harming their symbol of worship.
