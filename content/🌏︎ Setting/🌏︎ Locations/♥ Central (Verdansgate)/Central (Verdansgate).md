### About
Verdansgate (known by most people as central). This is the unofficial capital city of the dome, and is where most of the trading and white-collar jobs take place.
It has a tiered system, where the poorest and most expendable are sent to the lowest tiers, which are sites of mining and manual labour- awful conditions that make life unbearable for those who are forced to stay. The richest, most valued members of society are in the highest tiers- the top tier being the palace of which the royal family live in. There are 13 tiers, although the bottom 4 are inhabitable long term and have no living space. These tiers span from the base of the ravine- an awful site full of toxic fumes and horrific monsters- to the spire of what looks like an industrial mountain, the castle sitting at the very top as if it were reaching for the sun itself. 
Due to the absurd depths the ravine reaches, many of the lower tiers are far larger than the upper tiers, with each tier as you go down larger than the last. 

Tiers 13-10 are the bottom of the city, which reaches the base of the ravine. The lowest tier is flooded with monsters, and many high level adventurers spend time here clearing out large swathes of land- typically for mining excursions who dig up the extremely valuable magisteel deposits. Usually the larger the deposit, the more monsters are around. The next few tiers up decrease in monsters and quality of ore, but a lot of adventurers spend time here. For this reason, the lowest tiers are actually better furnished than the 7-9 tiers. 
This is offset by the toxic fumes, which if someone were to spend time in without a mask would slowly crack open their minds. This in short doses is reversible, and many starter adventurers cant afford the miraculous breathing devices that allow people to spend long times clearing out monsters in the lower tiers. For this reason newer adventurers often take short excursions to the 10th tier and head back to the surface. This is why many people in the 7-9th tiers don't descend for the better living conditions, as they would not be sane for long enough to make it last.  

Tiers 7-9 are for the lowest class, where disease is rife and crime is rampant. There are no schools here, and the only people who dares to enter are fools or the condemned. This is where the crime underbelly of the city thrives, and anyone on official business is swiftly taken care of. This isn't helped by the guards being the worst of the worst- the most corrupt and vile guards are posted to the lower tiers, where they either are out of sight, or are no longer an issue. 

Tiers 4-6 are the middle class and commoners. They often have jobs in factories and fields around central, and are thought of as the expendable proletariat who does as they're told for cheap. This is far less inhospitable to human life, with the 6th tier being the tier below the crust of the ravine- still seeing a lot of sunlight throughout the day. 

Tiers 1-3 are for the rich and upper class. The third tier being smaller noble families who have less of a name for themselves, yet still often act in haughty, stuck up manners. The second tier is where discrimination and snobbishness is geared up- a lot of the nobles here are reputable families who have large amounts of land outside of central. They usually have a manor or mansion in central for show, with many strange pieces of architecture being displayed here in an attempt to show off extravagant wealth.

The top tier, Tier 1, is where the royal family and highest noble families live. Common people are not allowed to step in these areas without a permit or invitation, and they must be escorted by a group of guards. Nobles may travel without a group of guards, but they still require an invitation or permit.

### Known for
Central city of the world. A hub of trade and crafts, basically any service or product can be found *somewhere* in Central, not necessarily for the best price. 
The royal family also lives here, although many proceedings take place in [Splitpoint](Splitpoint.md).

### Trade + Exports
Anything can be set up to be traded in Central. The main Merchants guild building can be found in Tier 3, as well as the main Adventurers guild building
