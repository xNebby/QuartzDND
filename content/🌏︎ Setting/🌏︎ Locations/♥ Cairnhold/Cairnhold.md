### About
Cairnhold - A dwarven city of epic proportion residing under one of the mountains in the great mountain range. This city was abandoned approximately 2000 years ago for unknown reasons, but the previous generation of dwarves (~300 years ago) have moved back in and made it their home.

The city is in a huge man-carved cavern spanning a a few square miles of space. It has many caves and caverns dug from this main body, with the dwarven castle being nestled in the opening of the second largest cavern. The actual city has a maze like pattern of streets and crannies, with grand buildings connecting into almost a web-like structure. There are larger main streets that have replaced buildings, forming a large mostly grid-like pattern for efficient transport. 

---
### Known for
Dwarven folk are known for their hardiness living so close to the mountains, and this is reflected in their wares. Cairnhold is well known for its incredible foundries - Being capable of forging great machines out of the strongest of Magisteel alloys. While these foundries have produced some of the greatest human-made tools, Adventurers love the so called "junk" they export as well, many fighting tooth and nail for even a single tool from the trash heap. These rejects are often made by dwarven apprentices testing out designs in a cheaper, lower grade metal. Dwarves often scoff at these, but even these tools are capable of lasting up to 10 tall-men generations (or just 2 dwarven generations). A finely made dwarven tool can easily last a millennia.  

Metallurgy is not all Cairnhold is known for however- Dwarves have found the best way to pass the moonless nights under the mountain is with a good drink. Cairnhold is one of the best quality exporters of mead and alcohol- a lot of the highest quality booze being refined over the whole lifetime of a dwarf. This obviously does not come cheap, and is mainly bought by nobles and the upper class.

---
### Trade + Exports
A wide range of artisan tools. Accounts for the majority of high end adventurers gear. A wide range of magic items, both for adventuring and daily life, can be found here. Metals and alloys can be forged into high grade bars here, ready to be used by tinkerers who live outside of Cairnhold. A lot of industrial parts are made in Cairnhold, and are shipped to be put together elsewhere.
