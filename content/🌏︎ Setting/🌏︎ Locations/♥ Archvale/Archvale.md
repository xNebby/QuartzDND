### About
Archvale is the capital city of magic. It is the centre of all things to do with magical artifacts and powers, and is a hub for up-and-coming mages for many different reasons. 

The three major universities of Auraxia can be found in Archvale (Aurelia, Krylian, Drakkin). While all three have their specialty subjects outside of the magically inclined, they all regularly compete to prove who is the best of the three mage schools. 

Archvale has a very distinct architecture, a lot of its buildings being made not out of brick and mortar, but carved out of stone using the minds eye of the mages living there. Due to this, it can be a little... eccentric at times. There are regularly strange, floating islands where houses were meant to be, or N-Dimensional towers who seem to both do and don't exist. 

To keep the material plane from ripping into shreds from this mighty vortex of magic, there exists a Magic Regulating field around all of Archvale- This barrier acts to suppress magic in certain areas while monitoring the usage of it in others. This barrier might work well to prevent fighting between mages, but  is huge inconvenience to other refined mages. Many can apply for permits for "barrier free" zones around certain technical implements, just to prevent the barrier affecting any readings from said zone. For most people, the barrier does not seem to be visible, and has no physical bearing- however for the more arcanely adept they may see a glimmering, translucent film that passes through any material and shifts around currents of magic. Moving through this barrier can feel a little stifling for extremely competent mages. 
Many more adept mages find this an infuriating restriction on something they find as natural as breathing, and as such have moved out of Archvale to wherever the wind takes them. 

Archvale has a big adventurer scene also- It is known to have fresh graduates and dropouts looking to join parties for cheap, so a wide variety of skill levels reside here. For this reason Archvale also has a very successful nightlife scene, both from students showing off their skills and adventurers looking to spend their coin.

### Trade + Exports
Magic items! Archvale has a lot of intricate and complicated magic items that are exported. These can scale from common all the way to very rare- depending on if a student or a master made it. These can be things that are extremely useful to the average person, or weapons designed for combat and adventurers. While these are typically very powerful, a lot of the mages of Archvale do not typically have the best idea of how they would be used in battle, and as such they can be recklessly explosive in power. 

[Spell Quartz](../../Homebrew/Items/Spell%20Quartz.md) is a very valuable export from Archvale- one time usable spells that come in the form of a crushable quartz. These are a more expensive alternative to spell scrolls. 
Spell scrolls are extremely valuable too, as many wizards and mages sell them in their spare time to help fund their degrees.
