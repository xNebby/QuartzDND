### About
This university of magic has ties to the dwarven kingdom, and is attended by a large quantity of artificers.  

---
### Known for
This prestigious school uses their wide range of technological know-how to create high quality magic items as well as magical applications that affect the quality of life of nearly every citizen in the inner dome. 

---
### Trade + Exports
- High quality magic items.
- Convenient tools used in wide ranges of applications.
- Contributions to public infrastructure and private sector industries.
