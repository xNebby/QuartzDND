### About
Scorgate was constructed at one of the ravines ends. It was built between a huge split mountain, which the ravine passes through cleanly- the ravine tapering out to level land just past the mountains edge. The huge dual peaks either side of Scorgate often block out the sun for weeks at a time, with both peaks being some of the tallest you can find around the mountain range. The cuts down the middle of the two peaks was once famously clean cut- however erosion took its toll, and now the mountain edges have slowly started to whittle away into jagged, slanted land.

Scorgate is the gateway between the inner ring and the outer ring ([The wastelands](../../../../The%20wastelands.md)), and as such a huge wall has been constructed to prevent many of the desert beasts from ravaging the inner land. For this reason Adventurers often meet up at Scorgate- there are always quests to be found here for clearing out monsters, and due to the immense presence of adventurers the immediate area around the wall is fairly clear. 

An important thing to note about Scorgate is the climate- The wastelands at this side of the dome are barren terrain with harsh sun that burns any plant-life trying to develop here to a crisp. Despite this, the desert dunes are teeming with life- The issue being most of it is monsters looking for an easy meal. 

There is a sparse forest developing on the inner ring side, however it is a frail and weak forest, with many of the trees being sickly and stunted due to the environment. For this reason, Scorgate has to import a lot of its food and resources. The reason this is economically viable is due to the large quantities of [Iridescence](../../Homebrew/Items/Iridescence.md) found in the wastelands. This magical substance is used all over the place for power production as well as crucial components in many potions. It is pretty much exclusively harvested from Scorgate, and adventurers are often stationed at outposts dedicated to extracting as much of the fluid as possible.  
There are also strange large monoliths of purified magisteel found inside the mountains of Scorgate. These strange structures are colossal in scale, with a small fraction of its surface area visible from the inside of Scorgate. Mana surveyors have estimated that many more of these monoliths exist around the mountain range, but due to the depth and scale of them it is difficult to reach them typically. 
Due to the nature of pure [magisteel](../../Homebrew/Items/Magisteel.md), the excavation efforts to remove chunks of it are slow and laborious. This has created a huge industry for mining, where a single fragment of this pure metal could pay an entire months wages for a miner. This is because only the strongest of miners are capable of breaking off fragments. 

Another important note about Scorgate is the Outpost, about 12 hours travel from the great wall of Scorgate. This is the place many adventurers travel to in order to set off into the forest. It acts as a stepping stone to allow adventurers to set out into the desert, avoiding the days worth of travel for every time they wish to reach a decent amount of monsters. This outpost has allowed adventurers to keep the area immediately past the wall of Scorgate (mostly) monster free. There are still the occasional group of beasts that have been disturbed by adventurers, who roam towards Scorgate in a futile attempt to flee.
