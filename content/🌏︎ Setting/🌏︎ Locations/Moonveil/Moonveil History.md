### History of [Moonveil](./Moonveil.md)

The name Moonveil comes from how the whale would glide through the sky- softly glowing due to a gift from [Aurelious](../../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Deities/The%20gods/Current/Aurelious.md), the God of the Sun. When the whale glided over a civilisation, its softly glowing white underside could often be mistaken as a second moon. 
Legends say the Whale of Moonveil was granted this gift by [Aurelious](../../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Deities/The%20gods/Current/Aurelious.md) so that they could always see each other as they played across the skies- day and night. The legends say Aurelious loved to bounce across the clouds, causing mischief and joy across the lands. They say he loved to spend time with the Sky whales- while they looked graceful and serene they also loved to play, and the younger of the whales, unburdened by a heavy island, would often fly swiftly through the sky with Aurelious. 

The legend also states that another god- [Nyx](../../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Deities/The%20gods/Current/Nyx.md), the mother of the night- grew jealous of watching Aurelious prance around the skies with the serene creatures, and wished to draw their eyes to herself. She cast a curse onto the most favoured of the beasts- a curse designed to weaken and sap its strength, forever placing the weight of its shadow upon it. She faced Aurelious with an Ultimatum- to let the whale live a long life, slowly and peacefully passing away as it loses it strength, or to continue to spend time with it- slowly burning away the whale and its lifespan by his very presence alone. This brought despair to Aurelious, who hated to watch his friend wither slowly. 

This is when Aurelious granted the gift- He plead to the god of crafts, [Brixburn](../../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Deities/The%20gods/Current/Brixburn.md), to bottle some of the suns essence into an idol- one that his friend, the sky whale, could hold forevermore to keep the curse at bay. Brixburn begrudgingly agreed, in return for some spare essence to fuel the heart of his great forges. Aurelious was overjoyed, and swiftly accepted the deal- trading a part of himself to save his friend. The idol was placed on the back of the whale, allowing the deep, sharpened shadows to recede to their normal lustre. 
The whale was overjoyed to feel this immense weight relieved- it looked everywhere for its friend, but no matter how far it travelled or how much it cried the old songs they would sing together, it could not find the god of the sun anywhere. 
This is when the whale was given the name "Moonveil"- The idol's soft glow emanating from the whale's whole body. The legend goes that if you spend enough time near where the idol is, showing care and affection for the whale- you too can hear the joyful laughter of eons passed- echoing the voice of a lost god. 

This glow lasted up until recent times (Approx 40 years ago)- when a member of the [Dawning Sun](Dawning%20Sun.md) Family took the precious relic from the town of Moonveil- the people powerless to stop this cruel feat of barbarism due to the heavy presence of the family's royal guards. The family stated they had a claim to the relic due to their Namesake, which they were given their name due to a distant ancestor (roughly 1400 years ago) being a child gifted to the people by the Aurelious. As the previous King, [King Pleides](../../../../King%20Pleides.md), was in the pockets of these nobles, he ruled in their favour, and allowed their blatant theft to go unpunished.
