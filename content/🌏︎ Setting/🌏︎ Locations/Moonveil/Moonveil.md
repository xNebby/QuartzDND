### About
Moonveil is the largest civilisation that can be found atop the [Sky Whales](../../Homebrew/Creatures/Sky%20Whales.md). It spans over a pod of 15 Sky whales, out of which 7 are large enough to be permanently habitable. Moonveil has a long and storied history- with the main body of the town being located atop the back of a roughly 1700 year old Sky whale. The other main whales that are lived on are younger, around 1400 years old.  ''
The people on Moonveil tend to crops and keep livestock like anyone else- more importantly however, they have to care for and tend to the sky whales they live with. They form a symbiotic relationship with the whales, where they take care of parasites and creatures trying to leech off the whales (typically involving clearing large barnacles from the side of the whale) and occasionally tending to its health, and in return the whales take care of the majority of the dangerous creatures that would threaten the towns safety.

### Trade + Exports
Moonveil is fairly self-sustaining. They have crops and livestock that they grow on the land found on the whales back- they use the fresh rain and salt water to purify into drinking water, and the trees that grow in the forestland the whales sometimes pick up is enough for them to build houses- They even have a culture of bees that allow people to make mead. 
The main thing that they import is new reading material- Books and tomes are always welcome to go into the library. They also import more exotic foods, with hardier alcohols being a big import. They do sell some of their harvests, but the most profitable industry in Moonveil is the tourism. People love to go on a long-term holiday on the sky whales, with an option for shorter (but typically more expensive) trips using [Sky Riders](Sky%20Riders.md) to gracefully glide down from atop one of the whales.
