There isn't much known about how the world was ruled before the dome existed, but what little people do know hints at legends of other kingdoms in the dome that unified into the singular kingdom that exists in the modern day. These kingdoms represented various races and after the events of the domes formation, they united under the banner of the Kingdom of [Auraxia](../Overview/Auraxia.md).

Power initially settled in the form of a unified council. This council was seated by the rulers of the various kingdoms, and particularly important nobles. In times of struggle, this council structure was slow and ineffective- bureaucracy and deliberation between various rulers with their own vested interests meant that the people experienced tragedy after tragedy with their lords simply bickering.
This discontent from the people grew, as they watched their crops fail and their land lords grow fat from wealth. This lead to the first of the great restructures of the kingdom, where the people bound together under a banner of a new king, who supplanted the council and acted as a decisive way to navigate nationwide disasters and issues. The council was kept, but the previous rulers descendants had been incarcerated and most of them publicly executed for their poor decision making. The people instead instated local representatives, who could actually speak for their individual needs.

This system worked at the beginning, but as people grew accustomed to it, the power hungry did as they do, and wealth and greed slipped its insidious claws into the minds of many of these nobles. A cycle quickly formed, where a noble would be chosen by the people to represent their needs. This noble would gain reputation and wealth, and be well acclaimed by the people who gave them this position. Then over the next few generations, their families would grow greedy and corrupt, turning on the very people who gave them their power. This of course would typically end the way corruptness usually does. With a public execution and a speech of how next time would be different. Only the exceptionally corrupt or the exceptionally honest families survived this period, which led to the next great restructure.

Over time, these families came to understand that their self fulfilling cycles would eventually bring their own downfall- and as such, they would often pass down businesses not necessarily by direct blood, but by merit and provable capability. This did often include the most worthy of a business being married into the original noble family, but if that wasn't possible then the family would bestow the last name upon the new heir. 
This prevented noble families from growing fat from their own wealth, and forced heirs to become capable descendants to the business in their own right. This concept was also applied to structures like the royal family- The previously appointed head of the council, the king, had to be strong in both will and body. 
While this position was often kept in a certain bloodline for a few generations due to training children from a young age for battle, it never could be locked down by any one group of people. There were of course requirements to be even considered for these- one had to be of noble blood to be even permitted into the selection.
