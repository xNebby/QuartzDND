The kingdom of Auraxia has persisted for what some say is roughly 2700 PD (past dome), but it has seen many restructures throughout this time, and as such many people cannot give an accurate figure for how long the dome has existed, and even if there was ever a time before it existing. 
The current record of time keeping states the world to be in the year of 1038 in the age of iron (IA), with the notion of a new age gaining traction as many people are calling it the golden age of mankind. This is in reference to the slaying of [Lord Etherax](../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/The%20Dragons/Lord%20Etherax.md) by [Aeternus](../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Royalty%20&%20Nobles/Aeternus'%20Party/Aeternus.md) and his party, marking an end to the [Revolution of Fire](Revolution%20of%20Fire.md). This is the most recent restructure of the kingdom, and changed much about the Kingdoms [Ruling](./Ruling.md). 

The age of iron began after the [War of iron](War%20of%20iron.md), where the seven armies of humanity clashed for who would become the ruler of rulers. The [War of iron](War%20of%20iron.md) was brought to a close due to interference from the God [Aurelious](../../%E2%98%BB%20Characters/%E2%98%85%20NPCs/Deities/The%20gods/Current/Aurelious.md), who hated to see humanity slay themselves. The species of humanity were given capitals for them to rule over, united under the banner of [Auraxia](../Overview/Auraxia.md)- Continent born of the sun.
While tall-men were given the central city, elves were given a sacred land north of the land of the giants, while dwarves were sent to reclaim their birth home of Cairnhold- abandoned for a Millenia due to reasons nobody can determine.
