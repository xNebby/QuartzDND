### About
The merchants guild has many different benefit tiers. These typically scale with how big of a business you own, and get more exclusive depending on how much money you make and put through the guild. 
### Tiers
The Benefit tiers go like so:
- Greenhorn
- Novice
- Journeyman
- Adept
- Veteran
- Master
- Baron
- Lord
It is important to note that the Lord title does not necessarily mean one is with the merchants guild, but it is a way that many people have become nobility.
