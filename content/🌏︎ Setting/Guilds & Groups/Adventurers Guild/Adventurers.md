### About
The adventurers guild has different ranks of adventurer. These ranks are used for pricing and danger level of quests, with more dangerous quests going to higher rank adventurers.

### Tiers
The ranks of adventurers go (in ascending order):
 - Bronze 
 - Silver
 - Gold
 - Platinum
 - Jade
 - Adamantium
 - Magisteel
