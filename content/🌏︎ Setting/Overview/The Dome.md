The dome is all that is and all that ever was for the people of Auraxia. It has existed for as long as many people can remember, and those who do remember are rare to come by. Many people believe a time before the dome never existed, and it was simply the discovery of the domes existence that made people come to believe the world had been constrained- their perceived visage of the sky being eternal simply the mortal mind being unable to comprehend the enormity of the world.
