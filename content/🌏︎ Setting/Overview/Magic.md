### About magic:
Wizards typically use latent magic in the environment, while sorcerers have their own source of magic- Warlocks of course source their magic from patrons. 

A measurement of magic is called magicules - Where magic is put into quantised packets of raw energy. Mages at Archvale have calculated that there are 6.02x10^23 base units of energy per one magicule, with many a wizard attempting to visualise this number going insane. There is a tale of Mage Kirtbux the third (One of the former teachers at Drakkin) visualising this number in its entirety. He proceeded to use chalk to cover his entire auditorium with dots- only stopping on the third day when he collapsed from exhaustion. Ever since, people have been happy to keep the number at an approximation - Kirtbux's Constant. 

An important thing to note about magic is its function in monster physiology. Monsters usually feed off of and grow depending on how much latent magic is in an area, meaning they tend to congregate around large sources of magic- This explains why a lot of monsters show up around rare resources and materials, and why there are so many monsters near the basin of the ravine. 

### Learning magic:
The average person could theoretically become a wizard if they studied enough- however the cost of that studying is a major roadblock to the common person (Not including the fact of how difficult the studying is), with many requiring a backing from a noble family in order to attend even one of the minor academies in Archvale. These backings usually result in the prospective mage working for the family for the rest of their lives. 
People may think then instead that getting a patron and becoming a warlock is a far easier route to developing magical capabilities, however many patrons in this world are far less than noble and will always tend to operate for their own benefit- at the expense of their benefactor. Many people will die or meet fates crueller than death far before they meet a patron who has a semblance of morality or free will. 
This makes being a sorcerer the "easiest" way to know magic- of course if you're a sorcerer is pre-determined at birth, so, typically it is not the most accessible route to learning magic. 

Wizards becoming more adept includes refining their control of magic in the environment, allowing them to perform more intricate and complex spells. Sorcerers can use raw power instead of talent- using their own supply of magic to force spells into place. Warlocks instead work to deepen their bond with their patron, increasing the connection they have to the patron's power. 

Other types of magic user (which the 3 above would typically scoff if you referred to as mages) are typically on some spectrum between these three main ways of utilising magic. 

### The colour of magic:

- Abjuration: Blue
- Conjuration: Yellow
- Divination: White
- Enchantment: Pink
- Evocation: Red
- Illusion: Purple
- Necromancy: Green
- Transmutation: Orange
