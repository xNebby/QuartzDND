### About
Across the entire known world stretches a huge ravine that is rich in magical essence. This is where a lot of the quarrying takes place, and as such huge cities have been constructed both along and inside the ravine. This ravine stretches from one side of the mountain range to the other, with one side being notably deeper than the other. At either side of the ravine (in the cracks of the mountains) there are huge walls constructed that split the inner world from the outer world.
