### What are they?
Wild magic storms are strange, unpredictable fluctuations that can be caused by a selection of reasons. Natural storms often form around areas where natural magic can congregate, or where huge amounts of magic has been utilised by a spell. 

The storms can have various effects, and affect the probability state of any matter they come into contact with. These changes can be either temporary or permanent, depending on the scale of the storm. 

Anyone attempting to cast a levelled spell in a wild-magic storm must perform a d20 check for a Wild-magic surge. On a 1 they must roll a d100 against the wild-magic table. Certain creatures are immune to this effect (Namely creatures with anti-magic properties).

### History

The most infamous historic wild-magic storm took place at a place now called "Jewel of the sun". An entire dune in the wastelands was reconfigured by a passing magic storm into a town made of solid gold. The issue with this gold, however, is that due to the chaotic nature of the wild-magic storm it was left HIGHLY radioactive, and as such gives awful burns. The gold itself also bakes in the hot desert sun of the wastelands, reaching extremely high temperatures that persist throughout the night. As such it is impossible for anyone without some form of heat resistance to step foot in the lands without seriously damaging themselves. 

Despite these hazardous circumstances, Foolish adventurers often try to quarry some of the golden sand. There are a few dragons that have laid claim to this town of gold, and as such most adventurers do not manage to get away with much.
