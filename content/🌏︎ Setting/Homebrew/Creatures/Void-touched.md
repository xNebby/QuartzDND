The void-touched infliction is an aberrant curse/disease that is currently being researched by many major groups of interest. It has several effects on the creatures under its influence, and so far there have been no recorded cases of a sapient being coming under the effects of the infliction. 

The current stages of the disease include:
- Gaining an aversion to sunlight and water
- Becoming erratic and hostile
- Behave in unusual ways and gain almost a higher level of cunning- creatures that typically stay in dens start to ambush non-infected creatures.
- Creatures that are not infected by the disease are targeted by infected creatures. The infected creatures seem to be friendly to one another, even if they are typically predator and prey. 
- Gaining sections of their body that are somehow phasing out of existence- These sections of the creature are not visible, and its almost as if theyve been cut clean off. Looking at where the insides of the creature would be reveals a black void, with no light penetrating it. The area is completely missing, and can be passed through by other materials. 
	- If the section of this void is between two segments of the creature- for example the knee of a leg- the two segments can still move relatively to each other as usual. No motor function is lost, apart from any that relies on sections of missing limbs- i.e a creature would walk with a limp if their foot was missing. 
- Due to the volatility of creatures past this stage, it is often very difficult for researchers to discover more about the creatures unless they have died. 
- Very late stages of the void touched creatures often die due to unknown reasons. Some creatures at late stages of the disease display warped features with mutated functionality. 

Researchers are ongoing investigations into how this disease spreads. The disease is found extensively in the wastelands of scorgate, but it is possible to find diseased creatures further into the inner dome.
