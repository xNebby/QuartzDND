### Creature Description
Sky whales are creatures the size of entire islands- As podlings they start at around the size of a cow, and over their average of 3000 years lifespan they grow to be the size of islands. These magnificent creatures have the strange ability to float using the naturally forming runes that take shape in their fins. These runes are powered by ambient mana in the air and allow the whale to gracefully glide across the skies. These whales seem to be slow from a distance, but theyre capable of easily travelling 50 miles in a day. 

The whales often travel in a pod of up to 10 or more, with the largest whales typically leading. Many of the larger whales found in [Auraxia](../../Overview/Auraxia.md) have civilisations on their back- much like [Moonveil](../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/Moonveil/Moonveil.md) (The largest of such examples). 

The whales typically spend 2/3rds of the year in the sky. They then take 1/3rd of the year to rest, and descend into water. During this time they always stay near the surface of the water, so that any islands on their backs are not flooded.

The larger whales (roughly 1000+ years old) usually have large amounts of land and water built up on their backs- they have large ridges around the edges of their topside that prevent most of the islands mass from slipping off. These ridges also serve the purpose of retaining water for the whale- it drinks this water throughout the duration it spends in the sky. As the whales get larger, they are able to store far more water internally- allowing for more stable water levels on sky whale islands. 

These whales do still have blowholes, the breathing creates gusts of wind that people use to propel [Sky Riders](Sky%20Riders.md) into the sky, allowing them to traverse between whales in the pod.
Many people believe the whales to be fully sapient and capable of conscious thought- this is supported by the whale sounds that can be heard when one is to augment their hearing to be capable of picking up low frequency sounds. The whales have been heard communicating with each other in complex manners, and a clear social hierarchy has been found in their pods.

### Environmental effects
The whales often just snack on anything that passes in their way. They are usually peaceful creatures unless provoked, so they do not seem to partake in any hunting behaviour. 

The whales are often happy to have people on its back- This is because people usually develop a symbiotic relationship. They live off the land on the whales back, while in return they often clear the whales back of parasites / barnacles. The whales have been known to allow people to enter their mouths in order to help provide medical aid- one famous example was when a whale had a segment of its baleen broken, and communicated this telepathically to the people of Moonveil, stating its distress. several of the residents of Moonveil rappelled down into its mouth and repaired the section, alleviating its pain. This is also one of the examples of the whale's intelligence, proving it is capable of complex communication. It does not seem to communicate telepathically often. 

While the whales are typically very peaceful creatures, they have on occasion been seen to engage in combat with skyborne threats. Most notably is a report of a younger whale tearing a young adult dragon's wing off when it tried to claw at the whales eye. The dragon was seen plummeting to the ground, presumed dead.

### Habitat
The whales often roam around the northwest cardinal direction of the dome, tending to stay within a distance of 100 miles from the oceans of [Auraxia](../../Overview/Auraxia.md). They have been known to roam around the area of [Seatey](Seatey.md) when they are in the water, and often traverse over the inner ring perimeter to the beaches west of [Lake Ceres](Lake%20Ceres.md).
