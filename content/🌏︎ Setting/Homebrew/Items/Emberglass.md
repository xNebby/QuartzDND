### What does the item look like?
Emberglass is typically a refined form of glass infused with explosive fire magic. It usually looks like a very translucent amber, with the clearer the glass, the more refined the explosive typically is. 
Unrefined emberglass that has just been crystalised is typically quite cloudy due to impurities, as seen below.

### What does it do?
Emberglass is a stable explosive used by miners - large barrels of emberglass fragments can be found in abandoned mineshafts, depending on the circumstances they were abandoned in. 
Emberglass is very stable over long periods of time, akin to glass- it is valued by long lived races for this reason.

To actually explode emberglass, either a large impact or a primary detonation is required. Miners typically use a small explosive compound with a fuse to light the barrel and then leave the vicinity. 
The more refined the emberglass is, the more energy is contained within it, and as such the less stable it becomes (meaning less force is required to detonate it). For this reason, pure Emberglass or above is to be handled extremely carefully. 

Emberglass typically comes in 4 rarities: Dirty, Clear, Pure, and Perfect. Miners commonly use dirty emberglass (largely unrefined) as it costs far less.

### Where is it sourced?
Natural sources of emberglass are the best source for more pure clumps of the resource- being forged near volcanic activity or magma flows. The actual mining of the more refined ore is quite difficult however- as even in the natural world, pure emberglass (or much rarer perfect emberglass) can explode simply from a rock falling upon it. 
Several volcanic eruptions have been sourced back to emberglass exploding, and as such any natural veins of it are to be treated as extremely deadly. 

The safer, but more expensive, source is artificially manufacturing it-
The crystals are able to be formed in super saturated baths of fire magic, with the substrate being molten down gemstones. The longer the crystals are left in, the larger they grow- and they slowly force out impurities. The unrefined emberglass can be cooled and reheated to temper it. Large amounts of pressure help speed up the refining process, as it slowly pressurises the impurities out of the glass. The most dominant source of this production line is the [Magmar clan](Magmar%20clan.md) of the dwarves, who specialise in high heat and pressure productions.
