### What does the item look like?
Iridescence is a dark, slick liquid with a purplish hue. It glimmers faintly with a strange opaline shimmer, and smells strongly of death. To the touch it is slippery and yet simultaneously difficult to get off- it moves as if the liquid is one singular entity, flowing continuously whenever it is poured. It is worth more than its weight in gold, and where some is found, more often follows. 

---
### What does it do?
Due to the nature of the liquids stickiness, creatures that get a limb stuck in a part of the liquid often get pulled in- the more they resist and pull away, the more of a hold the liquid has on them. This makes pits of Iridescence deadly to fall into, and one would most certainly meet a dark, painful death if they were to be so unfortunate. This is made worse by the fact that it cannot be breathed in, unlike water- so any creature expecting to easily breathe air through water will be simply asphyxiated. 
There is evidence that this liquid produces fumes that slowly poisons anyone who inhales them- the same studies state that getting the liquid on you leads to this poisoning effect much quicker.
The liquid burns with a dark, purple haze- its heat is far greater than that of normal fire. 

All of theses properties can make iridescence quite difficult to harvest and contain- but in recent times people have made self siphoning pumps that when started, the natural properties of iridescence help pump it through into a container. For all the difficulties that iridescence provides in harvesting, it more than makes up for it with its exceptional properties. When purified and utilised correctly, the negative and harmful side effects of iridescence is nullified and instead provides a very useful substance for many different processes. This magical substance allows for many high end potions to be created- with hundreds of low end variants of these potions being made from watering down a single highly concentrated vial. This is not the only extremely valuable application- it can be used as a material component in high end magic items, and also is one of the key ingredients in synthesising purer variants of [Magisteel](./Magisteel.md)- Crucial for making some of the strongest magic items imaginable.

### Where is it sourced?
It is mainly found in [The wastelands](../../../../The%20wastelands.md) around the outer side of [Scorgate](../../%F0%9F%8C%8F%EF%B8%8E%20Locations/%E2%99%A5%20Scorgate/Scorgate.md).

### Other important details?
The merchants guild, seeing how valuable of a commodity Iridescence will be, has decided to privatise the processes of harvesting, purifying and utilising Iridescence, with permits required to do one of the above 3. This also includes requiring a permit for trading Iridescence. This was all applied under the notion of public safety, and prevents many smaller businesses from hitting it big by stumbling upon a deposit of iridescence.
