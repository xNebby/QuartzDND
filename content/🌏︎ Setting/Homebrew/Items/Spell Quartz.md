### What does the item look like?
Spell quarts crystals are small, semi-translucent quartz stone that has a soft glow attributed to the [colour](../../Overview/Magic.md#The%20colour%20of%20magic) of whatever class the spell inside is. They look brittle, and are easily breakable in ones hand. 

---
### What does it do?
Spell quartz are a disposable way of engraining spells into a one time use crystal. To cast the spell stored inside, you break the quartz. Higher rarity quartz is required for higher level spells. The strongest and most resilient quartz can only store up to a level 8 spell. 
Spell quartz is typically more expensive than spell scrolls, but more reliable. 

If the spell requires a saving throw or an attack roll, the spell save DC and the attack bonus is equal to the original caster's spell save and attack bonus (which will be written in the description)

---
### Where is it sourced?
Spell quartz can be dug up near large mana deposits, as well as can be purified and made using various types of rare gem by infusing them with [Iridescence](./Iridescence.md) and other miscellaneous materials. 

---
### Other important details?
A spell cannot be copied from a spell quartz. If Dispel magic is cast on the quartz, it loses the spell contained inside. A spell can be inscribed on an empty spell quartz over the typical time it takes to cast that spell.
